Questions

Do I need to download anything?

No. SAYON runs in your phone's browser. Add it to your home screen and it opens like any other app.

What if there's no signal under the house?

Record your notes anyway. They wait on your phone and go through as soon as you have signal again, in the order you recorded them.

Which standard do the reports follow?

Reports are laid out for timber pest inspections under AS 4349.3, including the areas you couldn't inspect and why.

Does SAYON decide what's wrong with the house?

No. You're the inspector. SAYON writes up what you say and you check every answer before it's sent. Anything structural is referred to a builder or engineer.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. Your company details and quote prices are shared across your team, and each inspector signs in with their own account.
