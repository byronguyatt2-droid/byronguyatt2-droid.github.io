Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

KORVUS Inspect ("Korvus," "we," "us") is a voice-to-report tool used by licensed timber pest and termite inspectors in Australia to record inspection findings and generate compliant inspection reports. This policy explains what personal information the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can contact us about it.

This policy applies to personal information collected through the KORVUS Inspect application, including information about property owners, occupants, and clients whose properties are the subject of an inspection ("clients"), as well as information about the inspectors using the app ("technicians").

Korvus is currently operated as an individual project, with formal business registration planned for the future.

What information we collect

Depending on how the app is used, we may collect:

  • Client and property information, including names, addresses, contact details (phone number and/or email address), and details about the property being inspected, as well as job and billing details such as inspection type, fee, invoice number, payment status, referral source, and any access notes (for example, gate codes) the technician records, as captured by the technician during an inspection.
  • Voice recordings and transcripts of the technician's spoken observations during an inspection, which may reference the client's name, property address, and details about the condition of the property.
  • Inspection findings, including descriptions of termite or pest activity, damage observations, conducive conditions, and recommendations, as entered or dictated by the technician.
  • Photographs of the property, where the photo capture feature is in use, which may incidentally include identifiable people, vehicles, or other personal property.
  • Signatures, captured digitally from the technician and, where applicable, the client, as part of the inspection sign-off process.
  • Technician information, including the inspector's name and any notes they enter while using the app.

We do not knowingly collect health information, financial information, or government identifiers (such as tax file numbers) through the app.

How we collect it

Information is collected directly through the technician's use of the app during an inspection — primarily through voice dictation, manual field entry, and (where enabled) photo capture and digital signatures. Clients do not interact with the app directly; their information is entered by the technician on their behalf, in the course of the inspection the client has engaged us to carry out.

Notice to clients at the time of inspection

Because clients do not use the app directly, the technician carrying out the inspection lets the client know, at or before the inspection, that KORVUS Inspect is being used to record and process inspection details — including that voice dictation is converted to text and structured using the third-party services described below — and makes this policy available to the client on request (for example, by sharing this page's link).

Why we collect it

We collect this information to generate an accurate, structured pest inspection report for the client, allow the technician to review and finalise findings before the report is issued, maintain a record of past inspections for the technician's own reference, and support compliance with the relevant Australian Standards governing timber pest inspections (AS 3660.2 and AS 4349.3).

How your information is processed, including by AI

Voice dictation is first converted from audio to text on your device. On the iOS app, this is done using Apple's built-in speech recognition service — this is governed by Apple's own privacy policy, not this one. If Korvus is used through a web browser instead (for example, on Android or desktop), the browser's own speech-to-text feature is used — for Chrome, this sends audio to Google's servers for transcription, governed by Google's privacy policy instead. Either way, this conversion happens before the transcript reaches Korvus.

The resulting text transcript is sent to a proxy server we operate (hosted on Cloudflare), which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API, a third-party artificial intelligence service, to convert it into structured report fields, and returns the result. This is an automated processing step performed by software, not a human reviewer. Cloudflare provides the infrastructure this proxy runs on but does not otherwise retain the transcript.

Some relevant facts about these steps, based on the relevant providers' published terms as at September 2026:

  • Data sent to the Claude API under commercial/API terms is not used to train Anthropic's AI models. API request data is retained briefly by Anthropic for abuse-prevention and operational purposes, after which it is deleted.
  • Speech-to-text processing is governed by whichever provider performs it on your platform — Apple on the iOS app, or your browser vendor (e.g. Google, for Chrome) elsewhere — under that provider's own privacy policy, not this one.
  • Because these providers' infrastructure may process data outside Australia, using the app involves an overseas disclosure of personal information to the United States or other jurisdictions in which these providers operate.

Where your information is stored

Inspection data — including drafts, saved reports, and any signatures captured — is stored locally on the technician's device, and is also synced to a cloud database (hosted by Supabase) so that reports are backed up and remain accessible if the device is lost, damaged, or replaced. This cloud sync means client and inspection data is held outside the technician's device, on infrastructure operated by Supabase and hosted in Sydney, Australia. Finished reports are provided to the client as a PDF document; once issued, the client is responsible for their own copy.

Who we share information with

We share personal information with:

  • Anthropic, to convert dictated transcripts into structured report fields.
  • Apple (on the iOS app, via its built-in speech recognition), or your browser vendor (e.g. Google, via Chrome's built-in speech recognition, if used through a browser instead), to convert spoken audio into text.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the proxy server that relays transcripts to Anthropic and back.
  • Supabase, which hosts the cloud database used to store and sync inspection data.
  • The client, who receives the finished inspection report containing their own property and contact information.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with any other third party for marketing purposes.

How long we keep information

Technicians may retain saved inspection reports, both on-device and in the cloud database, for as long as is useful for their records. We recommend technicians periodically review and delete reports that are no longer needed, particularly once a client relationship has concluded.

Security

Information captured through KORVUS Inspect is stored on the technician's device and in a cloud database operated by Supabase, which provides encryption in transit and at rest. Account-level sign-in is required to use the app, and access to cloud-stored data is restricted at the database level so that each technician's account can only reach its own data. When a technician signs out, their reports, photos and signatures are backed up to their account and then removed from the device. Technicians are also responsible for keeping their own device secure while signed in, since a lost or compromised device could expose locally stored inspection data.

Your rights

If you are a client and would like to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or ask a question about how it has been handled, you can contact us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests as promptly as we can.

If something goes wrong

If we become aware of a data breach involving personal information collected through KORVUS Inspect that is likely to result in serious harm, we will take reasonable steps to contain it, assess the impact, and notify affected individuals and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Children's information

KORVUS Inspect is a professional tool used by licensed pest inspection technicians and is not directed at or designed for use by children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, including as Korvus's business structure, technical features, or applicable law changes. The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy reflects the most recent revision.
