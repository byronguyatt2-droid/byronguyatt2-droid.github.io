Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Version 2

SAYON ("Sayon," "we," "us") is a voice-to-report tool used by licensed timber pest and termite inspectors in Australia to record inspection findings and generate compliant inspection reports. This policy explains what personal information the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and how you can contact us about it.

This policy applies to personal information collected through the SAYON application, including information about property owners, occupants, and clients whose properties are the subject of an inspection ("clients"), as well as information about the inspectors using the app ("technicians").

Who we are, and who is responsible

SAYON is operated by Byron Guyatt trading as SAYON (ABN 39 394 267 144), 7 Loftus Drive, Barrack Heights NSW 2528, Australia.

The inspector or inspection business using the app decides what client information to collect and what goes in each report. Sayon stores and processes that information on their behalf, to provide the app. Clients with a question about their report should contact their inspector first. They can also contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

What information we collect

Depending on how the app is used, we may collect:

  • Client and property information, including names, addresses, contact details (phone number and/or email address), and details about the property being inspected, as well as job and billing details such as inspection type, fee, invoice number, payment status, referral source, and any access notes (for example, gate codes) the technician records, as captured by the technician during an inspection.
  • Voice recordings and transcripts of the technician's spoken observations during an inspection, which may reference the client's name, property address, and details about the condition of the property.
  • Inspection findings, including descriptions of termite or pest activity, damage observations, conducive conditions, and recommendations, as entered or dictated by the technician.
  • Photographs of the property, where the photo capture feature is in use, which may incidentally include identifiable people, vehicles, or other personal property.
  • Signatures, captured digitally from the technician and, where applicable, the client, as part of the inspection sign-off process.
  • Technician information, including the inspector's name, email address, business details, licence number and any notes they enter while using the app.
  • App usage and error information: which screens and steps the technician uses, how long the AI takes, error messages and technical details of crashes, the type of device and browser, and the network address the app connects from. This is linked to a random account number, not to the technician's name or email, and never includes client details, dictation, report text, photos or signatures. The technician can turn it off in Menu › Data & backups.
  • Billing information for paid plans. Card details are entered directly with Stripe, our payment processor. We don't see or store full card details.

We do not knowingly collect health information, bank account details, or government identifiers (such as tax file numbers) through the app.

How we collect it

Information is collected directly through the technician's use of the app during an inspection — primarily through voice dictation, manual field entry, and (where enabled) photo capture and digital signatures. Clients do not interact with the app directly; their information is entered by the technician on their behalf, in the course of the inspection the client has engaged us to carry out.

Notice to clients at the time of inspection

Because clients do not use the app directly, the technician carrying out the inspection lets the client know, at or before the inspection, that SAYON is being used to record and process inspection details — including that voice dictation is converted to text and structured using the third-party services described below — and makes this policy available to the client on request (for example, by sharing this page's link).

Why we collect it

We collect this information to generate an accurate, structured pest inspection report for the client, allow the technician to review and finalise findings before the report is issued, maintain a record of past inspections for the technician's own reference, and support compliance with the relevant Australian Standards governing timber pest inspections (AS 3660.2 and AS 4349.3).

How your information is processed, including by AI

Speech to text. SAYON is a web app that runs in the device's browser. When the technician dictates, the browser's built-in speech recognition turns the audio into text. In Safari (iPhone and iPad) this is Apple's speech recognition, and in Chrome it is Google's. Depending on the device, the audio may be sent to Apple's or Google's servers for this. That step is covered by Apple's or Google's own privacy policy, not this one.

Optional audio transcription. If the technician turns on "Experimental: AI Audio Transcription" (off by default), a copy of the recorded audio is also sent through our server to Cloudflare Workers AI, which runs a speech-to-text model and returns a transcript. We don't store the audio.

Turning notes into a report. The text transcript is sent to a server we operate on Cloudflare, which forwards it to Anthropic's Claude API. Claude turns it into report fields and suggested wording, and the result comes back to the app. We don't store transcripts on that server.

The inspector decides. AI only drafts. The technician reviews every field, can change anything, and signs the report. No decision about a client is made by software alone.

Data sent to the Claude API under Anthropic's commercial terms is not used to train Anthropic's AI models. Anthropic keeps API data for a limited time for safety and operational purposes, then deletes it.

Where your information is stored

Inspection data — including drafts, saved reports, photos and any signatures captured — is stored locally on the technician's device, and is also synced to a cloud database (hosted by Supabase) so that reports are backed up and remain accessible if the device is lost, damaged, or replaced. This cloud sync means client and inspection data is held outside the technician's device, on infrastructure operated by Supabase and hosted in Sydney, Australia. Photos are kept in private storage linked to the technician's account, not on a public link. Finished reports are provided to the client as a PDF document; once issued, the client is responsible for their own copy.

Who we share information with

We use these service providers to run the app. Each one only receives what it needs for its job.

  • Supabase (database and photo storage, hosted in Sydney, Australia), to store and sync inspection data.
  • Anthropic (United States), to turn dictated transcripts into report fields.
  • Cloudflare (global network, including the United States), which hosts our server and, when it's turned on, the optional audio transcription.
  • Apple or Google (United States), through the browser's built-in speech recognition, to turn spoken audio into text.
  • Stripe (United States and other countries), to process plan payments. Stripe receives the account holder's billing details, not client or inspection information.
  • Sentry (United States), to tell us when the app breaks, and PostHog (United States), to show us which parts of the app get used. They receive the app usage and error information described above, not client or inspection information.
  • The client, who receives the finished report containing their own property and contact information.

Because Anthropic, Cloudflare, Apple, Google, Stripe, Sentry and PostHog may process data outside Australia, mainly in the United States, using the app involves disclosing personal information overseas.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for marketing.

How long we keep information

Technicians may retain saved inspection reports, both on-device and in the cloud database, for as long as is useful for their records. We recommend technicians periodically review and delete reports that are no longer needed, particularly once a client relationship has concluded. A technician can permanently delete their account at any time from Menu › Data & backups › Delete My Account. This removes their login, every report and photo stored in the cloud database, and everything stored on the device. A business owner must first cancel any active plan, and contact us to remove anyone else linked to their business.

Security

Information captured through SAYON is stored on the technician's device and in a cloud database operated by Supabase, which provides encryption in transit and at rest. Sign-in is required to use the app, and access to cloud-stored data is restricted at the database level so that each technician's account can only reach its own data. When a technician signs out, their reports, photos and signatures are backed up to their account and then removed from the device. Technicians are also responsible for keeping their own device secure while signed in, since a lost or compromised device could expose locally stored inspection data.

Your rights

If you are a client and would like to access the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or ask a question about how it has been handled, you can contact us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests as promptly as we can.

If something goes wrong

If we become aware of a data breach involving personal information collected through SAYON that is likely to result in serious harm, we will take reasonable steps to contain it, assess the impact, and notify affected individuals and, where required, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Children's information

SAYON is a professional tool used by licensed pest inspection technicians and is not directed at or designed for use by children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. We will tell technicians about important changes in the app or by email before they take effect. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version.
